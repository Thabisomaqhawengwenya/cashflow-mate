# CashFlow Mate – Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 4 November 2026 · **Effective date:** 4 November 2026

---

## 1. Who we are and what this covers
CashFlow Mate (the “App”) is a recordkeeping and cash-flow tool for small traders, made by **Maqhawe T Ngwenya** (“we”, “us”, “our”). This policy explains what personal information the App handles, why, where it is kept, who can see it, and what choices you have. It applies to the Android app, the web version and any related services we run.

We are the “data controller” for information we receive through our own servers. For records you keep only on your own device, you are in control of them, and we do not see them.

---

## 2. The short version
- **The App is built to work mostly on your device.** Your till records are stored on your phone unless you choose to use cloud features or the web version.
- We do not sell your data and we do not show advertising.
- The App does not automatically read your SMS inbox, contacts or location. You paste or import the information you want to record.
- AI features are optional. If you use them, some business figures are sent to Google to produce an answer.
- You can export, correct and delete your records, and you can ask us to delete anything we hold.

---

## 3. Information the App handles

### Account and profile
- Name, username, email address and phone number, city, business name, stall number, merchant till number, operating hours and a short tagline.
- A profile picture, only if you choose one.
- Your password. On our servers it is stored only as a one-way hash, not in readable form.
- Your till PIN, if you set one. It is stored on your device as a hash.

### Business records you create or import
- Transactions: amounts, dates, currencies, descriptions, references, counterparty names, categories and notes.
- Cash drawers and accounts (for example cash, EcoCash, InnBucks and bank).
- Customer credit and supplier debts, which can include names, phone numbers, amounts owed and repayments.
- Inventory, invoices, contacts and receipts, if you use those features.
- CSV files you import and SMS text you paste in so the App can read it.

### Settings
- Your language, currency and exchange-rate settings, and your own Google Gemini API key if you add one.

### Information about other people
- Customers, suppliers and, if you use the reporting features, people you report for suspected fraud (name, phone number, ID or reference number, and what happened). See section 11.

### Support and technical information
- Messages you send to support.
- When you connect to our servers, basic technical information such as your IP address, device or browser type, and error and request logs.

### What we do not collect
- We do not read your SMS inbox automatically, access your contact list, or collect your location or advertising identifiers.
- We do not process your payments or hold your money, and we do not connect to your EcoCash, InnBucks or bank accounts.

---

## 4. Where your data is stored
- **On your device (default):** In the mobile app, your records are stored in the App’s local storage on your phone. We cannot see or recover them. If you lose the phone or clear the App’s data without a backup, they may be gone.
- **On our servers (web version and optional cloud sync):** If you use the web version, or turn on cloud sync where it is offered, your account and records are stored in our database and are tied to your account so other users cannot see them.
- **Backups you make:** The App can export your records to a backup file. These files are not encrypted by the App, so please store them somewhere safe.

---

## 5. How we use your information
We use it to:
- create and run your account and show you your records, totals and reports;
- read, categorise and de-duplicate the SMS text and CSV files you import;
- provide optional AI explanations and suggestions;
- check licence keys and unlock paid features;
- keep the App secure, fix errors and answer support requests;
- meet our legal duties.

We do not use your records to build advertising profiles.

---

## 6. AI features and Google Gemini
If you use the AI features:
- The App sends a question and a set of figures to Google’s Gemini service. These can include totals, category breakdowns and, depending on the feature, recent transactions, debts and drawer balances.
- Where the App asks for your own Gemini API key, requests are made with your key and are subject to your agreement with Google. In the web version, requests may go through our server.
- Our web and server versions are designed to remove phone numbers and identity details before sending data to AI services. Even so, please avoid typing personal details about customers into AI questions.
- How Google handles the data is governed by Google’s own terms and privacy notices.

**AI is optional.** If you never turn it on, none of your records are sent to Google.

---

## 7. Who we share information with
We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:
- **Service providers** that help us run the App, such as hosting and storage providers, Google (for AI), and app distribution and build services (for example Expo and app stores). They may only use it to provide their service to us;
- **Authorities or courts**, when we are legally required to, or need to protect rights, safety or security;
- **A buyer or successor**, if the App or our business is transferred, in which case this policy will continue to protect your data or you will be told of any change.

We do not share your records with EcoCash, InnBucks, banks or other traders.

---

## 8. Our legal basis
We process personal information under Zimbabwe’s Cyber and Data Protection Act [Chapter 12:07] and similar rules, relying on:
- your consent, for example when you turn on AI or cloud features, or add a profile photo;
- performing our agreement with you, to provide the App you signed up for;
- legitimate interests, such as keeping the App secure and preventing misuse of licence keys;
- legal obligations, where the law requires us to keep or disclose information.

You can withdraw consent at any time by turning the feature off or deleting your data.

---

## 9. How long we keep information
- **Records on your device** stay there until you delete them or uninstall the App.
- **Records on our servers** stay while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we will delete or anonymise your data within **30 days**, except where we must keep something by law.
- **Server logs** are kept for **90 days**, then deleted.
- **Support messages** are kept for **12 months** after the issue is resolved.

---

## 10. Keeping your information safe
We use protections such as hashed passwords, signed login tokens, separation of each business’s data on the server, file-size and file-type limits on uploads, an optional till PIN with lock-out after repeated wrong attempts, and a privacy mask that hides figures on screen.

No app or network is perfectly secure. You can help by using a strong password and PIN, locking your phone, keeping the App updated and protecting your backup files. If we learn of a breach that affects you, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

---

## 11. Information about customers, suppliers and reported individuals
When you enter someone else’s details, you are responsible for having a good reason to hold them and for treating them fairly and lawfully. Collect only what you need, keep it accurate, and delete it when you no longer need it.

If you use the reporting features:
- record only facts you honestly believe to be true;
- do not include national ID numbers or other sensitive details unless truly necessary;
- remember that false or careless statements about a person can have legal consequences for you.

If you are the subject of a record and want to ask about it, contact us at `privacy@cashflowmate.app` and we will help where we can. Records kept only on a trader’s device are controlled by that trader.

---

## 12. Your rights
Subject to the law, you can:
- see and get a copy of your personal information (the App can export your records as CSV or a backup file);
- correct information that is wrong;
- ask us to delete your information;
- object to or limit certain processing, and withdraw consent;
- complain to the **Postal and Telecommunications Regulatory Authority of Zimbabwe (POTRAZ)**, which is the data protection authority, if you think we have mishandled your information.

To use these rights, contact us. We may need to confirm who you are first.

---

## 13. Transfers outside Zimbabwe
Some providers we use, including Google and cloud hosts, may process data on servers outside Zimbabwe. When that happens, we take reasonable steps to make sure your information is protected to a similar standard, as the law requires.

---

## 14. Children
The App is not meant for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you think a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.
